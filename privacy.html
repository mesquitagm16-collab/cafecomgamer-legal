Privacy Policy

App: Cafe com Gamer Uploader · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Cafe com Gamer Uploader is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the Cafe com Gamer channel (TikTok @cafecomgamerr) to upload videos the channel itself edited to its own TikTok account.

What data we access

  • Basic TikTok account information of the channel owner (open ID, display name), only to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • The access token granted by TikTok, used only to upload and publish the channel's own videos.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect data from any other TikTok user.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and deletion

Tokens are stored only on the channel owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions), which deletes our access immediately.

Contact

Questions: send a message to @cafecomgamerr on TikTok.
